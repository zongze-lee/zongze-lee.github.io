Zongze Li

I'm a first-year M.Phil. student in Computer Science at Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Bo Han in the TMLR Group. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology (Excellent Class) from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2026.

My research focuses on foundation model reasoning: understanding what actually drives an LLM's reasoning, and making it stronger through training-time and test-time methods.

I am always open to discussions and possible collaborations. Please feel free to email me!
